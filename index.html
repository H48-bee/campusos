// how matching works

Three inputs. No dead links, no expired posts, no guesswork.

01

You describe yourself

Skills, interests, and experience level — typed in plain language, not a 40-field form.

02

The engine scores every listing

Each opportunity is tagged by the org that posted it. We score overlap against your skills and weight by fit for your level.

03

You get a ranked shortlist

Highest-fit opportunities first, with the exact tags that matched highlighted — so you know why it's there.

// for clubs, departments & companies

Post once. Reach every student it's actually relevant to.

No more flyers on notice boards or one-off emails that half the department misses. Tag your opportunity and the matching engine puts it in front of students who fit.

  • Free to post for campus clubs and departments
  • Structured tags mean better-fit applicants, less noise
  • Set a deadline and it auto-archives — no stale listings
